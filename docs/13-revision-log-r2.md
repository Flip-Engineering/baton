# 13 — Revision Log (review round 2: red/blue/explore)

*The red-team/blue-team/open-minded-critical pass (6 targets × attack→defend→explore-replacements, 18 agents) over the expanded design (docs 10–12, the capability modules, the specs). Every target returned **REVISE** — none KEEP-as-is, none REPLACE-wholesale, none CUT-entirely. That unanimity is itself the finding: the **mechanisms are largely sound, but the framing and the center of gravity are wrong.** This log records the verdicts and the changes they force, and it names the one reframe they converge on.*

## The convergent meta-finding: Referee, not Conductor

Four of the six explorers independently arrived at the same place: **baton's defensible product is the *Referee* — a cross-vendor, independent, hub-run verification + routing + cross-review layer — and the *Conductor* (driving and steering a live fleet) is an optional branch, not the headline.** The evidence is that baton *keeps discovering this and re-burying it*: doc 07 M1 already makes cross-review "the headline" and the eval the gate; doc 12 §3 already calls the hub "the judge"; doc 09 C1's "worker prose is non-authoritative, only re-run evidence counts" is already the whole thesis; doc 11 module 3 says "the load-bearing insight is forgeability." Then docs 10/12's paradigm prose re-centers everything on the Conductor and its moonshot vocabulary.

Why the Referee is the right trunk (paradigm-vendor-obviation verdict):
- **Vendor-proof.** A vendor will never build cross-vendor adjudication — a vendor grading itself against a competitor is the fox guarding the henhouse. Doc 01 §6: "the single-vendor boundary is exactly baton's reason to exist," and adjudication is the one thing that boundary *permanently* protects. The Conductor, by contrast, is being eaten now (agent teams, `codex remote-control`, the OpenAI plugin, native session-resume).
- **ToS-clean.** A Referee *consumes artifacts* (`codex exec --json`, `claude -p` output, git diffs, exit codes); it does not drive subscription-authed control loops — sidestepping the metered/contraband axis (doc 01 §7) that threatens the Conductor.
- **Bitter-lesson-proof.** Doc 12 §4's own rule — "scaffold WHAT and VERIFICATION, never HOW; the what/verify parts compound" — means the Referee *is* the verify half that gets *more* valuable as models improve, while the steering/HOW machinery is what a stronger model routes around.

**This does not delete the Conductor.** Reliable cross-vendor steering/interruption is real, unowned (ACP guarantees no preemptive steer — doc 01 §2), and worth building — but as an *optional branch gated on the eval*, not the identity. The supervisor's liveness apparatus (I1 fencing, I6 two-phase stop, the steering channel) moves from *headline* to *only-if-you-use-the-driving-branch*.

## Per-target verdicts and changes

### T1 · Mathematical validation & proof — REVISE (+ a live documentation lie to kill)
- **The lie, still in the load-bearing docs:** the "crown-jewel" wording implying a proof the hub's kernel accepts *is therefore valid-and-correct*. Kernel acceptance means **the proof matches the (worker-influenced) spec** — not that the code does what you meant. `sorryAx`/`native_decide`/`axiom` all pass a kernel; and worse, a worker who can't find a proof can **weaken the spec until a *true* proof closes** (kernel-green, genuinely valid, wrong property) — the cheaper attack the green merge-gate actively *selects for*. **Fixed in doc 11 module 3 and the module dossier this round.**
- **The deeper flaw:** triangulation (N independent formalizations must agree) does *not* solve autoformalization — N LLMs share training priors, so on an ambiguous spec they converge on the *same* wrong formalization and `agree:true` fires. "A perfect proof of the wrong theorem is worthless," and this is the *dominant* term precisely on the specs worth proving.
- **Change:** Split the module. **Bundle A (keep as identity):** the I7 evidence gate — hub re-execution in a fresh sandbox + hub-computed mutation score (`cargo-mutants`) + the fleet-wide, closure-keyed **counterexample corpus** (the one mechanism impossible in a single-user tool — the fleet's accumulated adversarial memory, applied stigmergically), with an **honest per-language rigor ceiling** as the headline (R0–R3 on most code, R4–R5 on Rust/C; R6 rare). **Bundle B (demote to earned-by-demand consumer):** autoformalization + Lean/Rocq/machine-axis — *consume* the AlphaProof/DeepSeek-Prover frontier, don't reproduce it; never emit "proven" over a worker-supplied spec. Rename the module from "proof" to **evidence**.

### T2 · Stigmergy at scale — REVISE (right instinct, wrong frame)
- **The frame is the bug, not the prose.** The mechanisms are a hub-mediated coordination *service* (claims serialize on a hub CAS transaction; the Board is a hub-owned materialized view) — the module's own critic names the honest lineage: advisory distributed locks + fencing tokens (Kleppmann), Bazel action cache, etcd/Consul/Chubby. That is **not** stigmergy (whose defining property is coordination *without* a central coordinator). Calling it "stigmergy/AIAI/O(N) — the bet" generates the defects both teams circled (miscounted O(N) scaling that dies under contention; an uncosted ~200-token perception poll the ant-analogy promises is free; non-transferable large-N robustness at N=3–10).
- **Change:** Rename T3 from "stigmergy is the bet" to "**mediated shared state, not broadcast messaging — a small-N coordination service (etcd/Bazel/Consul lineage), with partitioning (shared-nothing) as the primary parallelism strategy** and the Board a thin advisory layer above the worktree-isolation + I7-merge safety floor." **Keep** the two real wins: keep untrusted worker prose out of the orchestrator's scarce context, and "the medium is the record" auditability. **Trim `Scratch` ~10×:** delete `scratch_watch`/`scratch_heartbeat`/CAS-cells/free-text `scratch_post`; keep (a) an advisory lease slaved to the I1 worker lease, (b) presence auto-emitted on first scoped edit, (c) the conflicting claim **injected into the `Edit` tool result** so coordination is a byproduct of work the worker already does, not a poll it must remember.

### T3 · Emergence & the skill forge — REVISE (with a CUT inside)
- **CUT "emergence" as a design claim.** Emergence is an observation category, not an architecture — *detect* it (a hub-verified `capability.op` chain matching no designed template, kept only if the reuse counter fires), don't *engineer* it. Keep the uniform envelope as **interop** (per-vendor comparability), which survives without the emergence story.
- **Collapse the forge into the knowledge plane you already have.** The most-rediscovered thing in a coding fleet is not code — it's a **verified environment fact / command recipe** ("the test command is `mise exec -- mix test`, not `mix test`"): trivial to verify (I7's exact shape), enormously reusable, O(N)→O(1), and *vendor- and frontier-proof* (a stronger model still doesn't know your repo's test command). Ship that as a knowledge-plane promotion — **no `skill_*` subsystem, no trust tiers, no worker-facing search verb.** Demote code-skills, the Voyager loop, and CUA-distillation to earned-by-demand (they're the shrinking, most-dangerous 20% the bitter lesson erodes and the supply-chain threat model attacks).
- **The one thing:** the **applicability contract** — a verified-procedure record with a machine-checkable scope (`{repo, commit_range?, path_preconditions[], env_preconditions[]}`) checked at adopt/invoke against the adopter's live worktree. Without it, `verified` means only "passed in the author's worktree at commit X," and at fleet tempo the repo moves under the skill immediately, so "reuse" silently becomes "misuse."

### T4 · Architecture & buildability — REVISE
- **The two MVPs are disjoint** (roadmap M0/M1 builds a supervised for-loop; docs 10–12 describe a coordination-substrate moonshot) — that is red's FATAL and blue's "documentation-reconciliation debt," the same fact.
- **Change:** Declare the **control/verification plane THE product**; make the capability/knowledge/stigmergy planes *optional consumers earned by the eval*. Adopt **ACP as the stated default southbound tier** (socialize doc 07's "months-long, permanently-recurring" adapter-churn cost to Zed/vendors; own raw adapters only for the steer/usage delta). The capability plane is not cross-vendor-specific at all — it helps a single-vendor fleet identically — so bundling it with cross-vendor control is a category error that dilutes the one novel thesis.
- **The one thing:** **get one honest eval number before writing another line of capability-plane spec.** The entire corpus cantilevers off a single unmeasured fact — does a supervised cross-vendor fleet beat a single-vendor soloist on cost/time/quality? Doc 07 already front-loads this (M1: fleet ≤ solo pass-rate and >1.5× wall-clock → halt). Everything above the control plane is speculation until that number survives its pre-committed pivot criteria.

### T5 · Paradigm & vendor-obviation — REVISE (+ CUT rider on vocabulary)
- **The reframe (the session's strongest):** Baton-as-Referee, not Baton-as-Conductor (see the meta-finding above). Make verification/adjudication/decorrelation-measurement the trunk; the drive-a-fleet control plane an optional, API-key-default, best-effort branch on vendor-native primitives.
- **CUT the paradigm vocabulary** of docs 10–12 as load-bearing *pitch* — "beyond HCI," "three topologies," "stigmergy as the bet," "the emergence engine." Both red and blue concede these are retrofitted vocabulary over shipping prior art and an API contract; they inflate reviewer confidence without changing an adapter call. **Keep the two mechanisms that earn their keep as engineering rules** (not as a paradigm): *two channels never fused* (→ no `fleet_chat`) and *type every token by provenance*.
- **The one thing:** the **grader-provenance / independent-verification trust boundary** — re-verify (i) in a sandbox neither the worker nor its vendor controls, (ii) against a spec/grader the **human or orchestrator pins**, never a worker-supplied restatement. This is where a Referee's entire value lives and its entire failure mode (green-check theater strictly worse than trusting the worker) hides. Autoformalization is exactly this leak (T1).

### T6 · Cross-vendor ensemble economics — REVISE (right intent, wrong engine)
- **Separate the two N's.** pass@N = 1−(1−p)^N is overwhelmingly a *repeated-sampling* phenomenon (Large Language Monkeys, self-consistency): the lift comes from **sampling-N inside one vendor** (one auth, one adapter, one shared KV-cache — cheap), not from **vendor-N** (cross-vendor, cacheless, N× tokens + N× ToS surface). So best-of-N *justifies the opposite of baton* — it's best served intra-vendor.
- **Change:** Demote cross-vendor best-of-N generation out of the economics headline. The cross-vendor-*unique* value is **learned comparative-advantage routing** (1×, the `RouteStat` table) and **cross-review** (1×, proven, a different family catches your blind spot) — both ranker-free, both un-vendorable. Reserve cross-vendor best-of-N for the narrow honestly-N×-priced corner (unpredictable systematic per-family failure on a critical reverifiable task) and treat even that as **exploration that seeds the routing table**, after which the fleet stops paying N×. This is a one-paragraph retreat to the position the rest of the corpus already holds (doc 07 M1 marks `fleet_bakeoff` "optional"; doc 11 module 7 says "stop at the scorecard").

## What survives unscathed (the load-bearing core the reviews validated)

- **I7 hub-run verification** and the whole "worker prose is non-authoritative; only re-run evidence counts" trust model — this is the Referee's engine and every explorer built on it.
- **The supervisor invariants** (fencing, at-least-once cursors, two-phase stop, single-consumer approvals) — validated again as the Conductor branch's real content.
- **Provenance-typed context** (doc 12 §1b) and **two-channels-never-fused** (doc 10 §1) — kept explicitly as engineering rules.
- **The `BatonEvent`/`AciResult`/card normalization** — kept as interop (not as an emergence engine).
- **The honest scoping disciplines** already in the corpus (doc 07 eval-first, doc 11 "modules earned by demand," doc 11 module 7 "stop at the scorecard") — the reviews' prescription is largely *"do what your own scoping already says, and stop letting the framing contradict it."*
