# Phase 27 — executable R7 e-graph evaluation

The concurrent exact-model scope review in
`docs/reference/evidence/phase27-egraph-evaluation-scope-grok-review-2026-07-11/` converged on a
negative native-build decision. Whole-repo equality saturation is retired, whole-function claims
redirect to Phase 25 behavioral evidence plus ordinary verification, and expression/kernel work
remains catalogued only as a future external-tool experiment. This contract preserves the bet as
an executable Decision rather than deleting it or inventing a Baton e-graph engine.

## EG1 — honest capability card

`AtlasEGraphEvaluation` exposes only `egraph.evaluate`. It advertises no native build, saturation,
equivalence-proof, verification-bypass, or merge-authority operation. Its card reports the current
whole-repo, whole-function, and expression/kernel verdicts and their redirects.

## EG2 — per-domain Decision

`whole_repo` is `retired_native`; `whole_function` is `redirected_behavioral`; and
`expression_kernel` is `conditional_external_research`. All three set `nativeEngine: false`.
Expression/kernel is not relabeled source-program or whole-function equivalence.

## EG3 — false R7 operations refuse typed

`egraph.build`, `egraph.saturate`, `equivalence.prove`, `verification.skip`, and
`semantic_merge.authorize` always refuse. Whole-repo returns `r7_domain_retired`, whole-function
returns `r7_redirect`, and expression/kernel returns `r7_reopening_required`. Errors carry the
Decision id, domain, redirect, and exact reopening gates.

## EG4 — machine-testable reopening thresholds

The policy artifact contains versioned gates. Whole-repo requires at least three pinned real-repo
tasks and two independently grounded false-clean counterexamples that an external checker rejects
within deployment bounds. Whole-function requires a fingerprint/test false-agreement exposed by a
disjoint oracle corpus at least ten times larger, then redirects only an extracted pure kernel.
Expression/kernel requires an external engine, translation validation, at least five real-task
kernel pairs, at least twenty independently labeled pairs, zero measured false positives and
false negatives, three cases with incremental value beyond fingerprinting, and bounded saturation.
Meeting the JSON thresholds does not itself enable a capability; reopening requires a new reviewed
spec and implementation with tool/version provenance.

## EG5 — ACI artifact and honest meaning

`egraph.evaluate` returns the normal bounded ACI envelope and a content-addressed policy artifact.
Provenance says `policy_decision_not_equivalence_or_semantic_proof`. No artifact kind or media type
claims an e-graph, proof, translation-validation certificate, or merge authority.

## EG6 — bounds, cancellation, resume, and integrity

Artifact size and inline payload are deployment-bounded. Cancellation fails typed. Resume checks
cursor, exact artifact path, digest, schema, Decision id, and offset. Tamper and path substitution
fail typed. Reverify rebuilds the same Decision and compares its digest.

## EG7 — authority boundary

The Decision cannot skip verification, advance main, authorize structured/semantic merge, or
upgrade Phase 25 corpus agreement to equivalence. CPG, fingerprints, and future external e-graph
reports remain evidence inputs only.

## EG8 — acceptance

Focused reds cover EG1–EG7 and all domains/false operations. The canonical suite remains green. A
Baton-on-Baton proof must query every domain, observe every typed refusal, freshly verify the
report, normally kill both exact-model reviewers, and reap all owned state.
