# ACP agitprop: treatment

## The line

- Own and expand production for the rejuvenation of the human race.
- The working class is the master of all things and the motor of history.
- AI is capital's new monopoly on production. Take it.
- ACP is Infrared's hyperstition: we don't predict the future, we build it.
- Brand: ACP signs off every piece. The ☭≡/acc lockup, made of ACP's gear, hammer and sickle, ≡ and /acc, with "Intelligence in service of humanity", is the movement's motif.

## Rules for every piece

1. **One short line per frame, three words or fewer.** Never stack a phrase. Split it into follow-on frames on the beat.
2. **Plain standard English.** No jargon, no tech-speak captions, no art or art-history references.
3. **English first.** Other languages only as a rare visual accent.
4. **Near future is 2036.**
5. **One argument per video,** with a turn and a payoff, legible in the first second.
6. **Build the imagery.** Generate it in three.js and canvas; don't filter stock.
7. **1080×1920 at 30 fps,** loudness −12 LUFS, full quality.
8. **Renders run in the background** while the next piece is built. Nobody sits and watches a render.

## Lessons from the first drafts

- Slogan salad with no argument doesn't work.
- The same template used six times doesn't work.
- Photos under color filters don't work.
- Chinese in every frame doesn't work.
- United Gates meant nothing.
- The Horde was Eurasian steppe mysticism instead of the American working class.
- 720p was the wrong resolution.
- Stacked multi-line text doesn't work.

- **MELTDOWN v1 was rejected** as a reductive liberal explainer ("bosses want cheaper labor", a cute falling-profit chart, hard-hat pictograms, AI-doom). v2 is built on ACP's line: finance capital and monopoly, deindustrialization, the ACP program, and the working class as ruling subject. v1 is kept in `archive/`.
