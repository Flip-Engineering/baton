# Next pieces: concept exploration

These build only on ACP and Infrared's own idiom (see `../GROUNDING.md` and `../research/acp_lexicon.md`). Each piece gets its own visual system, not the same template reskinned.

---

## 1. 1776 / UNFINISHED

**Line:** 1776's values are realized only in socialism, so the revolution is unfinished and the Party finishes it.

**Frames:**
- 1776.
- THEY WON
- THE COUNTRY.
- THEN THEY SOLD IT.
- 2036.
- WE FINISH IT.
- ACP.

**Visual system:**
- A hand-inked parchment document, generated as procedural line work.
- Signatures burn away and the ink bleeds red.
- The parchment folds, origami-style, into the ACP gear, hammer and sickle.
- Frame-rate type set like a printing press: letters stamp in, one per beat.

**Sound:** fife-and-drum martial snare into distorted 808s. The same war-drum family as HORDE, in an American key.

## 2. NOT LEFT. COMMUNIST.

**Line:** the synthetic New Left is a superstructural enemy, an ally of the bourgeoisie that trades collective interests for the margins. We are not left. We are communists of the American people.

**Frames:**
- THEY SAID
- LEFT.
- WE SAID
- COMMUNIST.
- NOT THE MARGINS.
- THE MAJORITY.
- THE WORKING CLASS.

**Visual system:**
- A ruler-straight line on screen, the left–right axis.
- The class rises vertically off the axis: a column of people stacking into a skyscraper and then a rocket.
- The axis snaps.
- It pays off visually as "above the axis", not left or right.
- The target is the ideology (the synthetic left, the professional-managerial class), never groups of people.

**Sound:** half-time industrial with one enormous kick per bar. Silence on "LEFT.", full weight on "COMMUNIST."

## 3. EPOCH OF EMPIRES / BRING IT HOME

**Line:** close the bases, end the American Empire, rebuild the republic at home. The program literally says close all overseas bases.

**Frames:**
- THE AMERICAN EMPIRE.
- 750 BASES. The count is commonly cited but varies by source, so verify it before posting.
- ZERO FACTORIES.
- BRING IT HOME.
- BUILD HERE.

**Visual system:**
- The stippled ACP-red globe from RED WEDGE rotates.
- The base dots blink out one per 16th note.
- Each extinguished dot shoots a line back to America, where it lights a factory.
- The globe ends with America lit and the world dark but free.

**Sound:** a march tempo with snare rolls, a brass fanfare that detunes into a drone, and a triumphant final key change.

## 4. PEOPLE'S NATIONAL BANK

**Line:** abolish the Fed, found a People's National Bank, declare a Debt Jubilee, and direct credit to productive industry.

**Frames:**
- WHO PRINTS
- THE MONEY?
- NOT YOU.
- ABOLISH THE FED.
- A PEOPLE'S BANK.
- DEBT JUBILEE.
- CREDIT FOR
- PRODUCTION.

**Visual system:**
- A banknote engraving built procedurally from guilloche rosettes: spirograph line fields in ACP red.
- The Fed seal cracks and the engraving re-draws itself with the ACP mark as the seal.
- Dollar serial numbers roll to zeros for the jubilee.
- Credit lines, drawn as engraving strokes, flow into factories.

**Sound:** a cold minimal techno pulse, like a counting-machine click, that erupts into the HORDE war kit.

## 5. COMMON WEALTH (the heartland)

**Line:** common wealth, common prosperity. The heartland is the working class, and the working class is the master of all things.

**Frames:**
- THE HEARTLAND.
- THE FARMS.
- THE FACTORIES.
- THE RAILS.
- OURS.
- COMMON WEALTH.

**Visual system:**
- A three.js flyover of a procedural American plain: wheat fields as instanced grass, grain elevators, rail lines, a storm front.
- Lightning strikes on the kicks.
- The storm clears into a red sunrise.
- It shares the red riders' horizon, and the riders reappear on the ridge, tying it to HORDE.

**Sound:** the slow, heavy "tartarian" register. Throat drone and war drums at 80 BPM, then doubling to 160.
