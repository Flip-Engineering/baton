2What it qualitatively unlocks

The factory works

A spec-and-test-driven pipeline where the fleet does the authoring and the evidence is structural, not rhetorical. Nine contracts went through red-team→fold today; every one was genuinely unready, and every blocker named its fix. The chain catches real defects before impl — the cheapest place to catch them.

Trust machinery that trusts nothing

The attempt-echo attribution law refused an entire wave's harvest (0/5 markers) rather than misattribute — and recovery was a ten-minute pin-diff. Harvests refuse directories, wrong content, and unmarked work. Compliance climbed 0/5 → 5/5 → 4/4 → 5/5 as the frames learned; the structural pre-seed (#171) removes the class.

The collaboration layer is mapped, not assumed

PROVEN: message lane, reply chains, decision grammars, attention inbox, board, scratchpad read/elevate, wave observability. GAPPED with receipts: shared-write (#158), tight cells (#102), wake (#71/#181), quiescence (#163). And the store's sobering stat: 580 messages sent, zero query/steer/inform, zero decision events — the bidirectional lanes existed but had never been used. The lane-proof wave is exercising them now.

The coordinator pattern's three failure modes, found and filed

Blindness (#174 — members can't see siblings), semantics (#175 — the signal's roles read backwards by every spec author), lifecycle (#181 — the signal arrives after the coordinator's turn ends). One day of real use found all three; each has a filed, suite-pinnable fix. This is what dogfooding is for.

A filter for inspiration

The pm and dsh foundries didn't just find good ideas — they produced a rubric that rejects confidently. pm's confidence scalar and NLI cascade: rejected. Every wall-clock gate: vetoed or rewritten event-derived. The adoption list is seven items long instead of forty, and each carries its conditions.

The laws list became a spec

The campaign's standing-laws list — the traps the orchestrator had to remember — is filed as #184 with a full laws→machinery conversion map. The list's existence is the bug; its dissolution into admission refusals and driver scaffolding is the fix. Target shape: the laws shrink to pure methodology.

7The comparison verdicts, in one breath each

project-manager is a research journal with a query surface; baton is a work orchestrator with a control law. We take the journal half's shape — computed briefings, typed contradictions, belief states — and refuse its clocks, its SQLite locality, and its confidence scalar. "Activation before representation": machinery for a knowledge plane nobody writes to is machinery with no consumer.
DeepSeek Harness (in flight): everything-is-a-plugin on Cordis, with two ideas baton should steal with pride — model-visible means logged as a runtime invariant, and agent.inject() landing context in the next admitted request — and one trap to refuse: its primitives serve one live agent; ours serve a swarm.
