1What landed

Today: #79 — the worker delivery push shipped + closed

The BD3-C message lane: minted-sender delivery state per message (a delivery is bound to the worker's process generation — a reaped worker never receives a stale message), the in-flight trust-gate closure (#67 — the mid-flight kill class that murdered a healthy reviewer earlier in the campaign is structurally closed), and the control.delivery_* event family. Suite 32/32 twice, adjacents green, full gate accepted. This is the down-channel half of bidirectionality: a worker no longer polls for its answers — the orchestrator's messages arrive, with receipts.

Today: the foundry era — batch work as multi-member workflow specs landed + dogfooded all day

The bespoke driver is dead. Every batch job — contracts, red-teams, folds, suites, blue-teams, audits, comparisons — ran as one closed spec through the resident interpreter (#114's workflow-as-data), steering lanes declared in the spec. Seventeen waves, zero hand-written drivers. The day before, a wave cost the orchestrator a bespoke script and hours of driver bugs; today it costs a JSON file.

4What it qualitatively unlocks

The factory works

A spec-and-test-driven pipeline where the fleet does the authoring and the evidence is structural. Nine contracts red-teamed today; every one was genuinely unready; every blocker named its fix. The chain catches real defects before impl — the cheapest place to catch them.

From "orchestrator writes drivers" to "orchestrator declares workflows"

The foundry pattern (one spec, seventeen waves, zero bespoke drivers) means the orchestrator's act is now composition: members, scopes, steering lanes, harvest contracts. The #170 DSL then removes the JSON's verbosity — and #189 (auto-scaffold) makes even the spec materialize from the previous stage's harvest.

Trust machinery that trusts nothing

The attempt-echo law refused an entire wave's harvest (0/5 markers) rather than misattribute — recovery was a ten-minute pin-diff. Compliance climbed 0/5 → 5/5 → 4/4 → 5/5 as frames learned; the structural pre-seed (#171) removes the class entirely.

The collaboration layer is mapped, not assumed

Every lane now has a verdict with receipts: PROVEN (message, reply, decision grammars, attention, board, scratchpad read/elevate, wave observability), GAPPED (shared-write, tight cells, wake, quiescence), UNEXERCISED (query/steer/inform, decision events — until lane-proof). The map is the plan: packages ④ and ⑤ are exactly the gapped half.

The coordinator pattern's three failure modes, found and filed

Blindness (#174), semantics (#175), lifecycle (#181) — one day of real use produced all three, each with a filed, suite-pinnable fix. The coordinator+rows composition is the dynamic-workflow primitive; it now has an honest bill of health.

The laws list became a spec

#184 converts every agent-discipline trap (idempotency replay, base-commit captures, lock reaping, verbatim echo) into its machinery home — admission refusals and driver scaffolding. Target shape: the laws shrink to pure methodology.

A filter for inspiration

The pm and dsh foundries produced a rubric that rejects confidently: pm's confidence scalar and NLI cascade rejected; dsh's skills seam, kernel waterfalls, live fork, per-agent inbox rejected; every wall-clock gate vetoed or rewritten event-derived. Twelve adoptions survived with conditions, not forty without.

The knowledge plane's honest bill

Write-only today (13 entries, zero edges, zero elevations) — and now a landed plan that starts with activation (#186), not representation machinery. The landed-but-dormant 14-type edge vocabulary gets its first real writers from the foundry loop itself: folds emit Blocks/Resolves, QAs emit Supersedes, red/blue reports emit Attacks.

9The comparison verdicts, in one breath each

project-manager is a research journal with a query surface; baton is a work orchestrator with a control law. We take the journal half's shape — computed briefings, typed contradictions, belief states — and refuse its clocks, its SQLite locality, and its confidence scalar. "Activation before representation."
DeepSeek Harness: everything-is-a-plugin on Cordis. We take model-visible means logged as an invariant, the agent.inject() mid-flight context lane, and the adapter-contract discipline — and refuse the single-agent trap: their primitives serve one live agent; ours serve a swarm with fenced worktrees and content-addressed results.
