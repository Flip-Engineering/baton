# Grounding: who we are, and the client's corrections so far

Built from a full re-read of the conversation with the ACP organizer (NorCal org, social media and tech team), who has approval authority. Read it as background before working on a piece. It is an orientation, not a checklist (see `research/HANDBOOK.md`, "What changed and why"). The sources and verified quotes behind it are in `research/LINE_SOURCES.md` and `research/THEORY_CORPUS.md`; each piece's history is in `research/PIECE_NOTES.md`.

## Who we are

- **Eclectic American neo-Stalinists** rediscovering Marxism-Leninism under new American conditions.
- **Orthodox ML at the center,** with Hegel, Land, Negarestani and Dugin drawn into it, not the other way around.
- **u/acc and e/acc, modified** into pro-worker, pro-human Stalinist revolutionary material: **☭≡/acc**.
- **ACP is an Infrared hyperstition:** a party built to make the arrival of the future, the "neo-China" of industrial socialist power, *internal to America*, through American working-class struggle. We don't predict the future. We build it.
- **Infrared** is the ideological and aesthetic influence. **ACP / acp.us** is the brand, the call to action and the sign-off.

## How we speak (the rhetoric)

- **Associative, not literal.** Land, Dugin, Stalin and Hudson are *materials*, not labels we wear: we don't call ourselves "Landian" or "Duginist", and the ideas mostly come through image and rhythm. A verbatim quote can carry its author's name on screen when the piece calls for it (the client's answer, 28 Sep).
  - Land: meltdown; capital as runaway intelligence and search.
  - Dugin: the horde, the steppe, the land, tellurocracy.
  - Stalin: steel, the plan, industrialization, discipline.
  - Hudson: finance capitalism as rent extraction, "killing the host", Debt Jubilee.
- **Wary of binary formalism.** "They own X / we build X" tends to concede the other side's ownership as real. Ours is real; theirs is false and parasitic. That doesn't mean the owning class can't be named: naming monopoly finance and what it does is often exactly the point. The caution is against granting its claim.
- **Lean toward positive statements.** Say what we are and what we do, not only what we oppose.
- **Words and picture share the load.** Image and music carry much of the argument, but a sourced sentence is worth its frames: when a line runs long, split it across follow-on frames rather than cutting it to a slogan.

## What we say

- The working class is **the master of all things and the motor of history.**
- Own and **expand production** for the **rejuvenation of the human race**, through America, through the working class.
- Pro-production. Pro-AI. Pro-human.
- **AI is the monopoly of production** of capital, of search, of knowledge, of labor, arriving and becoming. The class takes it.

  Some context on AI, since several pieces turn on it. The client tends to treat AI less as a personified partner and more as a tool, a utility or a cybernetic system to be subordinated, or as the outcome of monopoly whose direction is decided by which class holds it: towards the dictatorship of the bourgeoisie or of the proletariat. He reads technofascism, and AI in its bourgeois form, through Dimitrov's account of fascism as the terrorist power of finance capital (see the Dimitrov section of `research/THEORY_CORPUS.md`). Subordinating AI rhetorically is part of what separates ☭≡/acc from e/acc and EA. It is a tendency of the whole argument, not a grammar rule: the Marxist tradition already speaks through machines and their tendencies (the "Fragment on Machines" and the 1859 Preface, THEORY_CORPUS §1.1 and §1.4), so a line where AI or capital acts is fine when the piece around it keeps class power and ownership deciding. The older bible framing of AI as a co-pilot or ally at the worker's side, and a "humans and AGIs" line wrongly credited to the client (it was another account's post, `r6_mining.md`), are not his view.
- **Class struggle and national rejuvenation.** America as the site where it happens.
- **The ACP program**, in its own words:
  - Debt Jubilee
  - abolish the Fed, and a People's National Bank
  - an Economic Bill of Rights
  - work in productive industry
  - an end to land speculation and monopoly

## Language

- **Plain, simplified standard English.** No clanker tech-speak and no academic phrasing.
- **A few words per frame.** When a line is long, split it into follow-on frames on the beat rather than stacking it or cutting it. There is no fixed word count; verbatim quotes keep their wording across as many frames as they need.
- **Avoid the DSA / progressive-liberal register** in our own coinages: the tone of "billionaires", "the 1%", "equity" talk, "cancel the debt" (we say **Debt Jubilee**). This is about register, not a word list: a verbatim source keeps its own words.
- **No artists and no art movements in the text.** No Groys, Malevich, Lissitzky or "People's Punk" references [CL69, CL101]. The look can stay; the lecture goes.
- **Chinese only as a rare aesthetic accent,** the way Japanese design uses English. It is not a second audience.
- **The near future is 2036,** not 2098.

## Brand and visuals

- **The ☭≡/acc lockup:** ACP's gear, hammer and sickle, ≡, /acc, and "INTELLIGENCE IN SERVICE OF HUMANITY", cut from the official banner. It is a motif throughout.
- **Every piece signs off with ACP** and acp.us.
- **Colors:** ACP red, black and cream. Globes are fine in ACP colors; the Infrared thermal globe is not.
- **Make the imagery well, by whatever means serves it:** three.js and canvas, print processes, the client's own poster art, archival and found footage (allowed since 28 Sep). Filters are no substitute for a real image.
- **Steppe and horde imagery is embraced for HORDE:** Tartarian steppe neo-Stalinists on the American plain, asserting their historical right as a class.

## Picture and sound are one organism

Music and picture can't be unrelated: the music is written for the picture's structure, not laid under it, and big picture events usually meet big musical ones. How they meet is the piece's own business. There is no fixed mapping of instruments to camera moves and no cut grid. Pacing should have tension and dynamism, which can mean fast cutting, a held explanatory shot, a long transition or a silence, whatever the argument needs at that moment.

## MELTDOWN (the concept)

Information-theoretic political economy:
- Monopoly is a **search function** in capital.
- Finance's search through technology **births AI**.
- The parasite tries to **freeze the superstructure** while **profit is destroyed**, and we enter **post-economics**.
- The freeze cracks. Everything melts to the class that makes it. Production after profit.

## The look

The client wants a distinctive house style aligned to the inspiration materials, and he has also warned against collapsing different concepts into one uniform look (correction 17). Both hold: the brand, the quality bar and the sensibility are shared, and each piece finds its own world.

- **Each concept keeps its own visual language.** HORDE is footage and steppe. RED WEDGE is poster geometry. UNFINISHED is parchment and ink. BANK is engraving. COMMON WEALTH is a 3D plain. MELTDOWN is its own world. Heat is one tool among many, not the house style.
- **What we reject:** the New Left, socialism with a human face, academic Marxism, Western Marxism, liberal individualism, and liberal and secular humanism. No moralist framing, and we don't wear these labels [CL214-215].
- **Not rigid literalism.** Art is associative and specific to our movement's history and outlook, never generic "Marxism".
- **Closers are short:** the ☭≡/acc lockup plus ACP.US, about 1.4 s. No verbose ACP card.

(The heat round's notes that stood here, full-bleed hot frames, the thermal HUD as house frame and a cut every 2 beats, were removed on 28 Sep: that round was rejected by correction 17 and the notes still read as live rules.)

## Production practice

- **1080×1920 at 30 fps,** full quality (the native iPhone resolution the client asked for). Masters stay at full quality; share copies are made separately.
- **Renders run in the background,** as the client asked [CL93]: start one and work on the next thing rather than waiting on it.
- **Iterate:** check stills, critique, fix, then render. Before calling a version done, watch the whole piece at full length and compare it with the previous version and the strongest earlier one: a new version should keep or deepen what they said and how good they looked.
- **Don't delete work.** Superseded versions go to `videos/archive/`.
- **The project lives in `/home/user/acp-agitprop`.** Commit locally. ~~Never push anywhere~~ **Reversed** (reconciled 28 Sep: the client asked "please push to a remote repo on my github, make one for it" [CL238]; the mirror `github.com/flip-engineer/automated-content-pipeline` exists for this, pushed via `videos/scripts/sync_repo.sh`. The client separately ruled never to push to `whargis/Blog-Posts` [CL49-51], which stands. See `HANDBOOK.md` §3.7, §8.1.)
- **No supercuts.** The client sees no benefit in stringing the videos together [CL48, CL61].
