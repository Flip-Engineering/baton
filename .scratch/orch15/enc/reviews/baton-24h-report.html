07Frictions (feeding the AX program, issue #10)

Control-surface divergence. runs.start records goal/plan and parks at awaiting_plan_approval; waves.start auto-approves and reports a third vocabulary (stopped / start_failed / work_completed) over the same machinery. Three phase vocabularies for one lifecycle — the case for one unified control surface, directly exhibited tonight.
Readiness honesty. Static-ready ≠ provider-alive, twice (401s found only at turn time). A bounded actual-inference readiness tier is owed.
Worker fixture discipline. One worker-authored time-bomb (real clock + fixed expiry) reached master green and failed three hours later. Contract text now mandates fixed clocks — worth a lint, not just guidance.
