# Reorientation (after correction 17)

This follows the research in `research/` (haz_infrared, acp_program_media, associative_materials, shortform_styles, client_references).

## What went wrong

- Round one had energy: full-bleed hot images and punchy lines.
- Then I "corrected" into literal explainers (icons, pictograms, "0.0%") and afterwards into one unified heat style. Both are wrong.
- My own old "lessons" contradicted the client. I called the steppe horde "mysticism" and banned filtered footage.
- The material is specific: Infrared, ACP, Haz's coinages, the program, American industrial history, the associative theorists. Generic Marxism is not.

## Principles now

1. **Every concept has its own world.** Different medium, palette, motion grammar and sound. No shared template beyond the ☭≡/acc closer.
2. **Specific over generic.** Use the movement's real material:
   - Infrared as "beyond the visible spectrum": the heat under the surface.
   - "The future belongs to the working class."
   - Debt Jubilee, the People's National Bank, re-denomination.
   - The Public Works Army; "cure, not profit".
   - 1776 as "a genuine people's revolution", and 2026 as its 250th year.
   - "CPUSA 2036 / we are in your party" lore, and 🦍☀️.
   - "AI will bring communism"; "civilization at the cliff's edge".
   - Magnitogorsk copied from Gary, Indiana; GOELRO; the *andurarum* river.
   - Neo-China arriving from the future.
3. **Associative, not literal.** No philosopher names, no ideology labels, no moralism, no explainers.
4. **Hook in the first 1.5 s.** Never open on a logo. Closer: 1.4 s ☭≡/acc plus ACP.US.
5. **≤3 words per frame, one line.** Most frames need no words.
6. **Picture and sound from one score.**
